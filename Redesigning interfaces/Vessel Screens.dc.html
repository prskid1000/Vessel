Vessel — the proposed screens

Drawn at 1 px = 1 dp on the target device: 421 × 927 portrait, 927 × 422 landscape. Every colour, size, radius and spacing step is a token from VesselTheme.kt.

Home does everything. There is one root and no bottom nav — a program is listed inside the container that owns it, so Apps is not a screen. The bottom edge is left clear for the taskbar. Short things are bottom sheets over home; only Files and the log viewer are pushes.
